# How to Play: The Guessing Game

## Game Objective

The goal of the game is to guess a secret number that the computer has chosen.

## Rules and Logic

1. **Number Range:** The secret number is always an integer between 1 and 20.
2. **Attempts:** You start with 20 attempts to guess the number.
3. **Gameplay:**
    * You enter your guess into the input box.
    * You click the "Check!" button to submit your guess.
    * The game provides feedback, telling you if your guess was correct, too high, or too low (this feedback appears where "Start guessing..." is written).
    * For every incorrect guess, the "Attempts Left" counter decreases by one.
4. **Winning the Game:**
    * You win by guessing the secret number before your attempts run out.
    * When you win, the secret number is revealed in place of the "?".
5. **Losing the Game:**
    * You lose if the "Attempts Left" counter reaches 0 and you have not guessed the number.
6. **Scoring:**
    * Your score for a winning round is the number of attempts you had left.
    * The "Best Score" keeps track of the highest score you have achieved across all rounds. A higher score is better.
7. **Restarting:**
    * The "Again!" button resets the game. This generates a new secret number, restores your attempts to 20, and prepares for a new round.

# How to Play: The Pig Game

## Objective

The goal of the game is to be the first player to reach a predetermined score, typically 100 points.

## Gameplay

The game is played in turns between two players. On your turn, you can perform two actions:

1. **🎲 Roll Dice:**
    * Clicking this button simulates rolling a single six-sided die.
    * The number you roll is added to your **"Current"** score for that turn.
    * You can roll the dice as many times as you want to increase your "Current" score.
    * **The Risk:** If you roll a **1**, your "Current" score is immediately wiped out to 0, and your turn ends automatically
2. **✋🏻 Hold:**
    * If you are happy with your "Current" score and don't want to risk rolling a 1, you can click "Hold".
    * This adds your entire "Current" score to your main score (the large number under your player name).
    * Your turn then ends, and it becomes the other player's turn.

## Winning the Game

The first player whose main score reaches or exceeds the winning total (e.g., 100) wins the game.

## Starting Over

* **🔄 New Game:** Click this button at any time to reset the scores to zero and start a new match.
